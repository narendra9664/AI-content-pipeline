Privacy, plainly.

Our website runs the same system we build for developers, so you can see it working on yourself. Here is exactly what it records, and how to switch it off.

Nothing about how you use the site is recorded unless you tap "Show me". If you do, the live panel shows everything we record, and "Stop tracking and delete my data" erases it with one tap. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we don't ask at all.

What we record, after you opt in

  • Which sections of the page you spend time on, and for how long
  • Whether you watch the film with the sound on, and how much of it
  • The buttons you click, the questions you open, and how far you scroll
  • The website or campaign link that brought you here, and whether you're on a phone or a computer
  • Your approximate city and country, worked out from your network. We never store your IP address.
  • A random ID kept in your browser, so a return visit is recognised

When you request an audit

We keep what you type into the form. If you opted in to the live demo, the summary of your visit is attached, so our reply and our call can be relevant to what you looked at.

Why we use it

  • To show you the live demo of our product
  • To write a relevant first reply to your audit request, and to decide who we call first

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to follow you around the web.

Who processes it

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores the data.
  • Google Gemini writes the first draft of our reply to an audit request. It receives your first name, company, website, monthly enquiry volume and a summary of what you looked at. It never receives your email address or phone number. We currently use Gemini's free tier, under which Google may use this content to improve its products.
  • Gmail sends our reply to you and a notification to us.

How long we keep it

  • Browsing data from visitors who don't request an audit: 30 days, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Audit requests and the visit summary attached to them: 12 months.

Your choices

  • Say "No thanks" to the banner. The site works exactly the same, and nothing is recorded.
  • Change your mind at any time with the "Live demo" link at the bottom of the home page. The panel it opens has "Stop tracking and delete my data".
  • To see, correct or delete an audit request, reply to any email from us or send a DM to @frontdesk.ops on Instagram. We'll do it within 7 days.
