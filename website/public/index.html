Questions

The things developers ask first.

Do buyers need to fill in a form for this to work?+

No. frontdesk AI reads behaviour such as repeat visits, floor-plan downloads and pricing views. That activity is tied to a named lead as soon as they identify themselves, for example through an enquiry, a WhatsApp chat or a link in an email.

Will the follow-ups sound automated?+

They are written for each buyer and mention what that buyer actually looked at. You set the tone and approve the messaging before anything goes live.

Does it replace my sales team?+

No. It takes the guesswork and the late-night inbox off their plate, so your agents spend their time with the buyers who are ready to talk.

Which channels does it work with?+

Your website, paid campaigns, WhatsApp and email. We confirm the exact setup for your tools and team during the audit.

What about privacy and consent?+

You can try it on this page: nothing is recorded until you tap Show me, you can watch your own score live, and one tap deletes it. On your website, tracking runs behind your consent banner, and before launch we walk you through exactly what is collected and how it is used.

What do you need from us to start?+

A short call and access to your website. The audit shows where the biggest gains are, and we agree a clear plan and timeline from there.
