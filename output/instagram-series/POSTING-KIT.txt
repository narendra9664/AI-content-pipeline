3. HOW TO POST
==============================

- Upload the MP4 as a Reel. Under "Edit cover", choose "Add from camera roll" and pick the matching file from the covers folder, for example covers/IG01-cover.jpg for IG01.
- The cover titles sit inside the area Instagram shows in the profile grid, so they stay readable there.
- Paste the caption from section 4. Each one has five hashtags, because Instagram now allows at most five per post or Reel.
- In the first hour, reply to every comment. On IG01 and IG09, reply with the answer and a question back. That turns a comment into a conversation, and conversations lead to DMs.
- Sound: each video has a music bed and sound effects. They come from your free ElevenLabs plan, which has no commercial licence. Before these run as business posts, either upgrade the plan, or add a track from Instagram's own music library in the Reels editor and turn the video's original audio down to zero.

IG07 · The reply that books viewings
Length: 24 seconds
Cover: covers/IG07-cover.jpg
------------------------------
Anatomy of a follow-up that books the viewing:

1. Her name. Never "Dear customer".
2. The exact unit she looked at, not "our project".
3. Something useful, based on what she looked at.
4. Two clear times to choose from, not "let me know".
5. A real person's name at the end.

Then send it in seconds, not tomorrow.

Save this for the next follow-up you write.
