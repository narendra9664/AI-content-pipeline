3. HOW TO POST
==============================

Instagram
- Upload the MP4, then under "Cover → Add from camera roll" choose the matching cover, for example covers/V1-cover.jpg for V1.
- Keep the cover title visible in the grid. Instagram crops the grid view to the middle of the cover, and the titles are placed inside that area.
- The burned-in captions already cover muted viewers.

LinkedIn
- Upload the MP4 natively; don't post a YouTube link, which gets less reach.
- Under "Edit thumbnail", add the matching cover, for example covers/H1-cover.jpg for H1.
- Under "Select caption", attach the matching subtitle file, for example captions/H1.srt for H1.

First comment
- Reply to early comments within the first hour. On LinkedIn, that early activity helps reach more than hashtags do.

6. OUTREACH: TURN THE VIDEOS INTO AUDIT CALLS
==============================

Posting alone won't fill your calendar. Sending the videos directly to the right people will. Aim for 10 personalised messages a day for two weeks. That's about 100 decision-makers.

Who to target
- Developers and brokerages with a launch that is live or coming up.
- Signs of paid ads, a website with floor plans, and an enquiry form or WhatsApp button.
- Contact the Head of Sales, the Sales Director or the Marketing Director, not a generic inbox.

Personalise in 3 minutes
Before you write, open their site and note one concrete thing. For example:
- the floor plans are behind a form
- there's no WhatsApp option
- enquiring takes seven fields
- there's no way to book a viewing online

That single line is what separates your message from spam. Don't send test enquiries under a false identity.

8. WHAT TO MEASURE
==============================

Use templates/analytics-tracker.md (in the GitHub repo) for each post. For this series, the numbers that matter are:

- Saves and shares: content people forward to colleagues reaches the decision-maker.
- Profile visits → website clicks: shows the hook created interest.
- DMs containing "AUDIT": the only number that is a lead.
- Audit calls booked, and held: the real goal.

After two weeks, keep the two best-performing hooks and make three variations of each. The code makes that cheap: new script, then voiceover, then align_vo.py, then render.
