## Example (Week 1 — Filled In)

```
Make a short about unqualified lead waste for luxury real estate developers.

CONTEXT:
We help luxury property developers automate lead qualification. Our AI system
checks new leads, scores buyer intent based on behavior signals, and routes
serious buyers to the best-matched sales agents. Target audience: developers
selling $2M+ properties.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Dark background (#0A0A0F)
- Primary accent: Electric Blue (#00A3FF)
- Secondary accent: Gold (#FFD700)
- Typography: Clean sans-serif, bold headlines
- Style: Animated infographics, data visualizations, flow diagrams
- Tone: Educational authority — teach, don't sell

THE HOOK:
"Your sales team is calling ghosts"

THE PAIN POINT:
Agents spend 80%+ of their time on leads who were never going to buy.
That's not sales — that's cold calling with extra steps.

VISUAL STYLE:
Motion infographic — animated funnel showing 100 leads pouring in,
97 fading to grey ghosts, only 3 glowing as real buyers.
Dark background, electric blue and gold accents.

THE 6-BEAT STRUCTURE:
Beat 1 (HOOK, 0-3s): "Your sales team called 40 leads today."
Beat 2 (SETUP, 3-10s): "They pitched, followed up, sent brochures. That's 6 hours of work."
Beat 3 (QUIZ, 10-18s): "How many of those 40 were actually ready to spend $2M+?"
Beat 4 (REVEAL, 18-28s): "Three. The other 37 were browsing. Your closers were working as receptionists."
Beat 5 (TWIST, 28-36s): "The 3 real buyers? They waited 4 hours for a callback. One already talked to your competitor."
Beat 6 (LOOP, 36-40s): "Your sales team called 40 leads today..."

RULES:
- Frame 0 IS the thumbnail. Make it visually striking.
- The last frame must seamlessly loop back to frame 0.
- Use word-synced captions (built-in feature).
- Include subtle sound effects for data reveals and transitions.
- No engagement-bait outros.
- Keep voiceover pace conversational — not rushed.
- End with quiet confidence, not a hard sell.
```

## After Claude Generates the Script

### Review Checklist:
- [ ] Does the hook make you stop scrolling?
- [ ] Is the pain point real? (Would YOUR clients feel this?)
- [ ] Is the data accurate? (Don't make up statistics)
- [ ] Does the twist add genuine insight?
- [ ] Does the loop feel seamless?
- [ ] Does the tone match your brand (authoritative but approachable)?
- [ ] Is there any language that sounds salesy? Remove it.
- [ ] Would YOU finish watching this if you were the target audience?

### Edit for Brand Voice:
Your reels should sound like a consultant briefing a CEO, not a marketer
pitching a product. If any line sounds like it belongs in a Facebook ad,
rewrite it.
